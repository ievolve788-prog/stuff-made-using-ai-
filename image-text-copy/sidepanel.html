Image Text Copier Settings

Google AI Mode shows up here

  1. Hover over any image on a web page.
  2. Click the small blue button in its top-right corner.
  3. This panel opens Google AI Mode, attaches the image and asks it for the exact text.
  4. The text is copied to your clipboard and you get a "copied ✓" note on the page.

Prefer a normal tab? Settings → "Open Google AI Mode in" → New tab.
