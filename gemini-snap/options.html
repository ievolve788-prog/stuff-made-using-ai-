Prompt templates

These appear in the dropdown right after you take a screenshot (first 9 also work with keys 1–9).

Quick send (⚡ Alt+Shift+Q)

Captures the visible screen, sends it to Gemini and presses Enter automatically. This prompt is sent with it (leave empty to send the image alone).

Where to open Gemini

Side panel needs you to be signed in to Gemini in a normal tab first. If the panel can't load Gemini, the extension falls back to a new tab automatically.
